Done! OmniMac is now in /Applications and has opened in the menu bar.

Next steps (first time only):

  1. Grant the Accessibility permission when it asks (⌘Tab by window,
     window shortcuts and pasting from the clipboard).
  2. Optional: Screen Recording, to see live thumbnails in ⌘Tab.
  3. In Settings › Notch, choose your music app (Spotify or Music).

Optional: closed-lid mode (Settings › Keep awake) is off by default. When you
turn it on, OmniMac explains what the administrator rule it needs allows and
asks your permission before installing it. And remember to turn it off before
putting your Mac in a bag.
