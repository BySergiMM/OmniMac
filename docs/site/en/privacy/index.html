OmniMac

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Privacy

OmniMac collects nothing. No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no ads. What you do with the app stays on your Mac.

What is stored, and where

  • Settings and preferences, in ~/Library/Preferences/com.seergiii.omnimac.plist.
  • Clipboard history: on your Mac. You can pause it at any time, clear it, and choose whether it survives quitting the app.
  • Saved window layouts and the album-artwork cache, in ~/Library/Application Support/OmniMac.

None of it leaves the computer, and all of it goes away when you uninstall the app.

When anything reaches the internet

Two things, and both are yours to decide:

  • Checking for updates. Once a day the app asks GitHub whether there is a new version. Turn it off in Settings, under "Check for updates automatically". GitHub logs that request the way it logs any download from its site.
  • Album artwork. When the notch shows what you are listening to, it downloads the artwork from the address Spotify or Music gives it. With no music playing, it never happens.

There is no other connection. Not at launch, not at idle, not while using the rest of the modules.

The permissions macOS asks for, and why

  • Accessibility: placing windows, ⌘Tab by windows, and the shortcuts.
  • Screen Recording: copying text from the screen (OCR) and window thumbnails.
  • Calendar: showing today's events in the notch.
  • Automation for Music and Spotify: seeing and controlling what is playing.
  • Audio capture: per-app volume and the equaliser.

Each permission is asked for when you use that module, and every module can be switched off. A module that is off asks for nothing and costs nothing.

This website

It is hosted on GitHub Pages. No cookies, no analytics, and nothing loaded from third parties: no external fonts, videos or social buttons. GitHub, like any server, logs the requests it receives.
