# Fichas para directorios de apps (copiar y pegar)

Todos requieren cuenta y los rellenas tú; aquí está cada campo listo. Los textos van en
inglés porque es el idioma de los tres sitios. Archivos que te pedirán:

- Icono: `docs/site/img/icon-512.png` (512×512) o `docs/site/img/icon.png` (256×256).
- Capturas (1362×464, inglés): `docs/site/img/en/notch-media.png`, `notch-headphones.png`, `notch-tray.png`, `notch-sound.png`, `notch-calendar.png`, `notch-timer.png`, `notch-performance.png`; menú: `docs/site/img/en/menu.png`.
- Web: https://bysergimm.github.io/OmniMac/en/ · Código: https://github.com/BySergiMM/OmniMac
- Descarga directa: https://github.com/BySergiMM/OmniMac/releases/latest/download/OmniMac.pkg
- Versión actual: la que diga https://github.com/BySergiMM/OmniMac/releases/latest (hoy 0.4.1) · Licencia MIT · Gratis · macOS 14.2 o posterior · Apple silicon e Intel.

**Descripción corta** (hasta 160 caracteres):

> Seven Mac utilities in one free, open-source menu-bar app: keep-awake, ⌘Tab by windows, dynamic notch, window snapping, clipboard, OCR and per-app volume.

**Descripción larga**:

> OmniMac replaces the small utilities you install on every Mac with a single native Swift app that stays out of the way: keep the Mac awake (with schedules, closed-lid mode and battery stop), ⌘Tab that switches between windows instead of apps, a dynamic notch with music controls, an AirDrop tray, today's calendar, per-app volume, a timer and performance charts, keyboard shortcuts and drag-to-edge window snapping with saved layouts, a clipboard history with images and files, screen OCR, a colour picker, a keyboard lock and a microphone mute key. When AirPods or Beats connect, the notch shows an iPhone-style card with the battery of each piece. Every module can be switched off; what is off costs nothing. Measured at idle: 0.017 % CPU, 19–27 MB of real memory and under one wake-up per second (methodology in the repository). Free, MIT-licensed, universal binary, no accounts and no telemetry. Interface in English and Spanish, following the Mac's language.

## Otros que aceptan envíos gratis (por si te sobra un rato)

- **ToolFinder** (toolfinder.co): igual que MacMenuBar, formulario corto.
- **Softpedia** (softpedia.com › Submit software): pide la URL de descarga y revisan a mano; da un enlace más y una insignia «100 % clean».
- **Product Hunt**: ficha completa en `docs/producthunt/README.md`.
- **Homebrew (cask oficial)**: cuando el repositorio pase de 75 estrellas o 30 forks, envía el cask a https://github.com/Homebrew/homebrew-cask (hoy solo está en nuestro tap: `brew install --cask BySergiMM/tap/omnimac`).
